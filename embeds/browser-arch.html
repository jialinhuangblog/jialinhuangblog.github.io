Processes vs threads: the three Renderer lanes are threads inside one Renderer Process; Browser, Network, and GPU are separate OS processes.
Note: events are spread out for readability. In a healthy app, all 36 typically fit inside one 16ms vsync interval. Each extra vsync the timeline crosses = one dropped frame.
